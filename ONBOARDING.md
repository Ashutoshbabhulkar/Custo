# CUSTO Business Onboarding Guide

## Onboarding Workflow

1. Navigate to `/onboarding`.
2. **Step 1 — Details**: Enter business name, URL slug, category (Healthcare, Hospitality, Services, Retail), contact number, city, and description.
3. **Step 2 — Branding**: Select primary, secondary, and accent colors, and add logo image URL. Inspect live card preview.
4. **Step 3 — Google Link**: Add official Google Business Profile review link and test.
5. **Step 4 — Launch**: Confirm details and access live public page at `/[slug]`.
