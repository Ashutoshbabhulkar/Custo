# CUSTO Product Roadmap

## V1 — Core Platform (COMPLETED)
- Customer Feedback + Review Assistant + QR Code + Multi-Tenant Admin Dashboard.
- AAS Hospital migrated as Tenant #1; ABC Dental Clinic onboarded as Tenant #2.

## V1.5 — Locations & Campaigns
- Multi-location business structure (e.g., OPD, Pathology, Pharmacy).
- Campaign-level response tracking and printable A4/A5 posters.

## V2 — AI Insights & Sentiment
- Automated sentiment classification (positive, neutral, negative).
- AI summaries of private feedback complaints & OPD waiting time trends.

## V3 — Reputation Management
- Direct Google Business Profile API integration.
- AI Review Response Assistant for drafting business replies.

## V4 — Subscriptions & Monetization
- Billing plans (Starter, Professional, Enterprise).
- Enterprise white-labeling & custom domains (`feedback.customer.com`).
